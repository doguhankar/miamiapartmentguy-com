# Miami Apartment Move-In Costs: The Real Numbers No One Tells You

**First month, last month, deposit, parking, amenity fees, keys, parking setup. Your move-in cost is probably 2-3x what you think.**

You find a one-bedroom for $2,500/month and you're excited. Then leasing hits you with the move-in sheet and suddenly it's $8,000 just to get your keys. Here's what actually happens.

## The Baseline (Almost Unavoidable)

- **First month's rent**: $2,500
- **Last month's rent**: $2,500
- **Security deposit**: $2,500 (sometimes $3,000 in luxury buildings)

Baseline subtotal: $7,500 before parking, before amenities, before anything else.

## Then Comes Everything Else

- **Amenity fee**: $150–$400/month. Some buildings call this a "community fee" or "facility fee." It covers pool, gym, lobbies, common areas. Month one: $150–$400.
- **Parking fee**: $150–$300/month in most of Miami. Month one: $150–$300.
- **Parking setup/gate key**: $50–$150 (one-time, sometimes waived).
- **Keys/fobs**: $25–$75 (one-time, sometimes included).
- **Valet parking surcharge** (if applicable): Sometimes charged separately at $50–$100/month.
- **Pet deposit**: $300–$500 if you have a dog or cat (non-refundable at some buildings).
- **Pet fee**: $25–$75/month (common in luxury buildings).
- **Parking deposit**: $25–$50 at some buildings (refundable).
- **Concierge/service fee**: Some buildings charge $50–$100/month for front desk services (increasingly common).
- **Furniture removal**: If the building handles move-out disposal, sometimes there's a $100–$300 charge.

## Real Example: One-Bedroom in Brickell

- First month: $2,800
- Last month: $2,800
- Deposit: $3,000
- Amenity fee (month one): $300
- Parking (month one): $250
- Parking setup/key: $100
- Pet deposit (if applicable): $400
- Pet fee (month one): $50

**Day-one cost: $9,700**. And that's not including furniture, moving truck, deposits to utilities, internet setup.

**Year-one cost**: $9,700 + ($2,800 × 11 remaining months) + ($300 × 11 amenity) + ($250 × 11 parking) + ($50 × 11 pet) = $9,700 + $30,800 + $3,300 + $2,750 + $550 = **$47,100** for 12 months of rent + fees.

## How to Cut This

- **Move during off-season** (May–August, January): Buildings offer concessions. Free first month, half-off second month. That's $1,400–$2,800 savings.
- **Negotiate parking**: Ask if parking is waived for 3 months or included. Some buildings will do it.
- **Skip the pet**: Adds $400–$600 day-one.
- **Rent out the parking spot**: Some people sublease their parking. You lose the cost after 6 months.
- **Ask about amenity fee reduction**: Rare, but some buildings reduce it for long-term leases.

## What's Refundable vs. Not

Security deposit: **refundable** (minus damages). Pet deposit: usually **non-refundable**. Amenity/community/service fees: **NOT refundable** (it's your monthly service, not a deposit). Parking fee: **NOT refundable** (it's a service charge). First/last month: **refundable as applied to final month**.
