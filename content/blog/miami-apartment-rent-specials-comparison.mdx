# Miami Apartment Rent Specials: Free Rent vs. Discounted Rent—Which Deal Actually Wins?

**Buildings offer "first month free" or "20% off." One saves more money. Here's the math and the hidden costs.**

It's summer. Buildings are desperate. Two offers:

**Option A**: "First month free + half-off second month"
**Option B**: "Move-in special: 20% off rent for 12 months"

Which saves more money? Which one bites you later? The math is specific, and the hidden costs are real.

## The Math: Option A (Free Month)

Advertised rent: $2,500/month. 12-month lease.

- Month 1: $0 (free)
- Month 2: $1,250 (half-off)
- Months 3–12: $2,500 × 10 = $25,000

**Total paid**: $0 + $1,250 + $25,000 = **$26,250 over 12 months**
**Effective rent**: $26,250 ÷ 12 = **$2,187.50/month**
**Savings**: $312.50/month = **$3,750/year**

## The Math: Option B (Percentage Off)

Advertised rent: $2,500/month. 12-month lease.

- 20% off = $2,500 × 0.80 = $2,000/month
- 12 months × $2,000 = **$24,000 total**
**Effective rent**: **$2,000/month**
**Savings**: $500/month = **$6,000/year**

**Option B wins by $2,250/year**.

## But Wait: The Hidden Cost of Option B

20% off rent sounds amazing, but there's usually a catch:

**Concessions often come with strings**:
- "20% off rent, but you must sign a 18-month lease (not 12)."
  - That's 18 months at $2,000 = $36,000 vs. 12 months at normal rate = $30,000. You're locked longer.
- "20% off, but parking fees are full price ($200/month, not waived)."
  - Option A might have waived parking for 3 months. Option B doesn't.
- "20% off, but you pay a renewal fee ($150) each year."

The leasing office will bury these conditions in writing. Read carefully.

## Real-World Comparison (With All Costs)

**Option A: Free Month + Half-Off**
- Rent savings: $3,750
- Parking waived month 1–3: $600 saved
- Amenity fee reduced month 1–2: $0 (they usually don't reduce this)
- **Total savings: $4,350**
- Lock-in period: 12 months (standard)
- Parking after month 3: $200/month

**Option B: 20% Off Rent**
- Rent savings: $6,000
- Parking: Full price immediately ($200/month, no waiver)
- Amenity fee: Full price immediately ($300/month)
- Lock-in period: 18 months (usually)
- Renewal penalty: $150/year
- **Total savings: $6,000 (but you're locked 18 months)**

If you were planning to stay 12 months:

**Option A**: $4,350 savings, 12-month commitment
**Option B**: $6,000 savings, BUT you're locked 18 months and paying 6 extra months at $2,000 + $200 parking + $300 amenity = $2,500/month real cost.

Option B costs an extra $2,500 × 6 = $15,000 for those 6 months. Your "savings" evaporates if you want to move.

## When Each Option Actually Wins

### Choose Option A (Free/Half-Off) If:
- You're unsure about staying 12+ months
- You want flexibility to move at 12 months
- The parking or amenity waiver actually applies
- You can move before month 3 to avoid paying parking

### Choose Option B (Percentage Off) If:
- You're genuinely staying 18+ months
- You don't care about parking waivers
- The 18-month commitment is actually your plan
- You're calculating true annual cost (not just exit cost)

## What to Ask the Leasing Office

1. "What's the effective monthly rent after all concessions?" (Make them calculate it.)
2. "Are there any penalties if I break the lease early?"
3. "Do concessions apply to parking and amenity fees, or just rent?"
4. "Is parking waived during any promotional months?"
5. "What happens when I renew? Are concessions still available?" (Often renewal rates are normal price.)
6. "What's the lease-break fee if I leave after 6 months?" (Usually 1–2 months' rent.)

## The Honest Recommendation

Free first month + half-off second month is a safer play. Less lock-in, clearer math, flexibility if life changes.

Percentage off is better if you're truly staying long-term, but read the fine print for extra lock-in or conditions.
